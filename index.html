Hi, I’m Terrs. I design things, make things, and constantly find new things I want to learn, because apparently choosing one creative skill and sticking to it was never an option.

I design digital experiences, but creativity for me doesn’t really stop when I close Figma.
I draw, craft, experiment, make things by hand, and regularly convince myself that learning one more skill is a perfectly reasonable use of my time and money.
I like details, colors, unusual ideas, and figuring out how things work.

Whether I’m designing an interface or making something completely unnecessary with my hands, the process is pretty much the same: get curious, try something, make a mess, refine it.

Do you wanna work with me? Have a project in mind?
I’d love to hear about it — let’s create beautiful things together.
