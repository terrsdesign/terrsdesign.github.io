01. Overview

Chocolate for Family is an ongoing UX/UI redesign project for an e-commerce platform serving both consumers and professionals in the chocolate and cocoa industry.

The project focuses on creating a clearer and more coherent digital experience for two very different audiences, while improving the way products, information and services are structured and communicated.

The process started with preliminary research, interviews and surveys to validate initial assumptions and better understand the needs of both B2C and B2B users. The case study will continue to evolve as the project moves through strategy, information architecture, interface design and testing.

02. The Problem

The current experience lacks clarity at a structural and communication level.

The website serves both consumers and professionals, but this dual audience is not immediately evident.

Users struggle to understand who the experience is designed for, how the different product lines relate to each other and where to find the content relevant to their needs.

At the same time, brand-specific naming and highly descriptive copy often require interpretation instead of supporting immediate understanding, adding friction to product discovery and navigation.

03. Proposed Solution

The proposed solution will be shaped by the validated research findings and added as the project moves into the design phase.
